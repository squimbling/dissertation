About me
I'm Dylan and I'm not a man. My mam was so sure I was going to be a boy she never thought to check, and chose the name Dylan. When I was ripped untimely from her vagina, emphatically female, she decided to keep the name. I bet she thought that was really funny at the time, but she's dead now, and it just means I spend a disappointing amount of time explaining to online strangers I'm actually a woman with a man's name. Not even a cutesy man's name, at that...

My favourite artist is Francesca Woodman. My favourite Muse album is Absolution. My favourite Absolution song is Falling Away With You.

I am 24(th century), probably part alien. I used to believe I was a starseed sent to Earth to suffer, but then I discovered Planet Internet and decided to reside here instead over worrying about whether I really fit in as an Earthling.

I live up North, in a seaside town where you're about as likely to have a sunny beach day as you are to find a happy person. We used to have a lot more ice cream shops and hippie head shops that reminded me of my mam, but they're all boarded up now. Not saying exactly where in case you're a stalker, but if you're from Sunderland area you know.

Passion is Art but currently working a dead end job. Open to commissions if you're stupid rich and take pity on a poor, broke graduate saving to move to London.

I collect cool pebbles I see on the beach and crystals. My sister Marnie used to tell me they fell from other planets. I still have a pet rock from when I was eleven.

Always open to new friends. Unless you're a bigot.

Woman, 23, killed in road traffic accident on A316

An urgent appeal has been issued by the London Metropolitan Police following a fatal collision on the A316. On 26 May, at approximately 22:58, an unknown vehicle crashed into a silver Peugeot Hybrid 2008, causing a collision into the underpass. The owner of the vehicle, named locally as Isabel Tiley, was pronounced dead at the scene.

Isabel, who was 23 years old and a Fashion student at Central Saint Martin's College, is believed to have suffered catastrophic brain injuries. She leaves behind her father, Ed Tiley, noted for his art dealings, and mother, Marisa Tiley-Renaud. "She had the sort of energy you could feel from another room," said Marisa, when asked for comment. "Her death will be felt far and wide, not least in our little family. "I worry I will never be the same person after losing her." Ed Tiley agreed with his wife's sentiments, adding: "I will never know a greater loss than today, and my grief is only worsened knowing how little information we have on my daughter's killer. "I pray my beautiful daughter's death will be bought to justice."

London Metropolitan Police have put out an urgent appeal for witnesses, citing the lack of information on the vehicle that caused the collision as a "serious concern".

"We are potentially dealing with a hit-and-run, and we need to find the driver of the vehicle that caused this collision," said a spokesperson for the London Metropolitan Police. ◀ back
